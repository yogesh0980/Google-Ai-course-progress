# 🧠 Google DeepMind: Build Your Own Small Language Model

This repository tracks my progress through **Google DeepMind: 01 Build Your Own Small Language Model** (part of the *AI Research Foundations* curriculum on [Google Skills](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/1341)). It includes my notes, lab code, and reflections as I work through the course.

## 📌 Course Overview

An intermediate-level, ~6 hour course covering the fundamentals of language models — from probability-based word prediction and n-grams through to transformer-based models — with hands-on coding labs and real-world case studies (including AlphaFold).

**Course link:** https://www.skills.google/course_templates/1341

## 🧩 Key Topics Covered

- Probabilities and randomness in next-word prediction
- N-grams and tokenization
- Limitations of n-gram models
- Transformer architecture (high-level)
- Responsible AI and ethical reflection
